Privacy Policy

Clavo — CBSE Exam Practice

Clavo is an independent practice app for students preparing for CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) exams. We do not require login or accounts.

The app loads practice questions based on the official curriculum and announcements from our servers over the internet. On your device, we only store preferences such as your last selected class (local storage).

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use ads or analytics SDKs in the current version.
